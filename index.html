Tokei

Watch Collection Log for iPhone

Tokei is a quiet, private home for a watch collection and for the simple habit of noting which watch you wore today. It is free, has no account and no ads, and nothing leaves your phone except to your own iCloud.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Replies usually within a few days.

When reporting a problem, the version number from Tokei's Settings screen and a description of what you did help most. Never send your export archive unless asked; it contains your collection.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your phone, in Tokei's own storage. If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy also lives in your own iCloud account, where only your devices can read it.
  • How do I back up? Tokei keeps the three most recent automatic backups itself. Settings ▸ Export collection writes a single file with every watch, photo and wear entry; keep it in Files or iCloud Drive. Settings ▸ Import restores it on a new phone.
  • Why can't I log a sold watch? A sold watch keeps its history but leaves your rotation. Un-sell it from its page if it came back.
  • Why does "cost per wear" hide its number? On purpose. Tap Show when you want to know.

Privacy

Tokei collects no data about you. The full statement is on the privacy page.
