Privacy Policy

Tokei: Watch Collection Log for iPhone. Effective 8 November 2026.

The short version

Tokei does not collect, transmit, sell or share any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of ours. Your collection, photos and wear log stay on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter, including watches, photos, purchase and sale details, wear entries and settings, is stored on your device in the app's own storage. You can export all of it as a single file at any time from Settings, and you can delete all of it by deleting the app.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your collection is also stored in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, so that your other devices signed into the same account can show the same collection. That data is protected by your Apple Account and is not readable by us. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy. Sync is off until you turn it on.

Purchases

Tokei may offer optional tips through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment details and no personal information from a purchase.

Photos and camera

Tokei asks for access to the camera or your photo library only when you add a photo of a watch. The photos you choose are stored with that watch on your device. The app never reads your library otherwise.

Siri, widgets, search and notifications

Logging through Siri, widgets or the Lock Screen is processed on your device. Watch names you choose to index for the iPhone's search stay in the on-device search index. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your phone.

Children

Tokei is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Since the app collects nothing, changes are unlikely to be material.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
